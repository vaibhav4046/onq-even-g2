Privacy Policy for OnQ

Application Name: OnQ • Effective Date: November 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how OnQ handles user information when you connect your personal accounts and run the OnQ copilot for Even Realities G2 smart glasses and the R1 smart ring.

1. Local-First Architecture & Zero Central Storage

OnQ operates locally on your own computer over loopback (http://127.0.0.1:8787). OnQ does not operate a central cloud database storing your Google tokens, emails, calendar events, files, or API keys. All OAuth tokens and connector credentials are encrypted on your local disk inside an AES-256-GCM encrypted vault protected with 0600 file permissions.

2. Google Workspace Data Accessed

When you authorize Google Sign-In, OnQ requests permission only for the features you connect:

  • Google Calendar: Reading upcoming events to prepare pre-meeting HUD briefs and creating or updating events only after explicit confirmation.
  • Gmail: Reading message threads for priority inbox digests and creating drafts or sending replies strictly after explicit user/R1 ring confirmation (Default-NO safety lock).
  • Google Drive: Reading document metadata/contents requested by you during active briefings or note creation.

Every write or external communication in OnQ is Default-NO gated: actions are staged first and executed only when you explicitly confirm via the UI or R1 ring double-tap.

3. Google API Services User Data Policy (Limited Use)

OnQ's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically:

  • Google user data is used solely to provide user-facing OnQ copilot features initiated by you.
  • Google user data is never sold, never used for personalized advertising, and never shared with data brokers.
  • Google user data is never used to train generalized artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

4. Third-Party Connectors You Configure

If you optionally connect Notion, Telegram, AssemblyAI, OpenRouter, Anthropic, or OpenAI, OnQ sends only the specific prompt, audio stream, or action payload required to fulfill your request directly from your local machine to those providers under your own configured credentials.

5. User Control, Data Deletion & Revocation

You stay in complete control of your data at all times:

  • You may disconnect Google Workspace or delete credentials anytime inside the OnQ Connectors Hub (which deletes the local encrypted vault entry).
  • You may revoke OnQ's Google access at any time from your Google Account permissions page at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
